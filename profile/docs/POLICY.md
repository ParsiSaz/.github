# ⚖️ Legal Compliance & Modding Policy

**ParsiSaz** adheres strictly to ethical modding standards and intellectual property regulations.

---

## 📜 Core Principles

1. **Strictly Non-Infringing**:
   - Repositories under the ParsiSaz organization do **NOT** distribute cracked executables, bypass Digital Rights Management (DRM), or host copyrighted proprietary binaries, audio tracks, or raw texture assets.
   - All patches require a legitimately purchased, legally acquired copy of the respective game or software.
2. **Fair Use & Transformative Localization**:
   - Localization assets, translated string tables, and font patches are transformative fan contributions designed to enable Persian-speaking users worldwide to experience interactive digital media in their native language.
   - Projects are published under open-source and creative commons licenses wherever permissible.
3. **Upstream Copyright Respect**:
   - All trademarks, characters, game logos, titles, and underlying codebases belong to their respective creators, publishers, and intellectual property holders.
   - ParsiSaz claims no ownership over upstream game content.
